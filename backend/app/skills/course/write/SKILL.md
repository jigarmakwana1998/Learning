# Delegate writing

1. Assign Writer a chapter or section through `task` with its outline IDs,
   objective, learner level, source IDs and relevant previous content.
2. Writer saves sections and returns their IDs and any research questions.
3. When more information would help, ask Researcher and give Writer the new
   source IDs and a revision brief.
